Mario Question Block Delivery Pad

A parcel-status pad controlled from a local phone page: arrived triggers an OLED animation and buzzer; collected clears the alert.

My roleHardware development and enclosure integration
HardwareESP32-S3 · OLED · buzzer · local Wi-Fi
Project stageDocumented project
