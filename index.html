03 / How I work

From design to a testable prototype.

My strongest work happens at the interfaces: where a board meets its wiring, a component meets its enclosure, or a design meets the physical test setup.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Map the system

Clarify the hardware blocks, components, connections, constraints, and the part I am responsible for.

02 / DESIGN

Develop the parts

Prepare PCB layouts, CAD models, wiring plans, or component references for the project at hand.

03 / BUILD

Integrate the hardware

Assemble, wire, program where needed, and check physical fit and electrical compatibility.

04 / VERIFY

Test and document

Run functional checks, record observations, and make the next build easier to reproduce.
